75% Subject-Wise Money-Back Policy

If none of the questions in the purchased subject's 10-set question-paper bundle matches the corresponding university examination, you are entitled to request a 75% refund of that subject's purchase price.

The Concept-Matching Clause: Examination questions may vary in exact numerical values, phrasing, diagrams, or units. A question is considered a match if the underlying concept, scientific principle, structural derivation, or method of solving is substantially identical. A numerical problem with altered figures but identical solution steps counts as a concept match.

If at least one question matches the examination paper, no refund is available for that subject. All refund decisions are evaluated individually per subject by reviewing the actual question paper uploaded as evidence within the claim window.
