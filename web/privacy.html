Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

To deliver digital study bundles and process potential refunds, we collect the following student information: Full Name, Academic Roll Number, Email Address (for Google Drive access), Mobile Phone Number (for order SMS alerts), and Student UPI ID (collected solely for 75% refund disbursements).

2. Payment Information Security

All financial transactions are processed securely through our PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment partner, Razorpay. AuraExam does not collect, store, or have access to your card numbers, CVVs, netbanking passwords, or UPI PINs.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to:
• Provision private Google Drive viewer access for your purchased PDFs.
• Transmit transaction receipts and customer service updates.
• Verify student roll number and process 75% concept-matching refund claims.

4. Zero Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade student personal information or academic details to any marketing agencies or third parties.

5. Grievance & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding data privacy or wish to request data deletion, contact our Privacy Officer at support@auraexam.com.
