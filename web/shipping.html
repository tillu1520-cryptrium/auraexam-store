Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. 100% Digital Delivery Model

AuraExam exclusively sells intangible digital educational products (electronic PDF documents). There are no physical items, books, or parcels shipped, and no physical shipping charges apply.

2. Delivery Timeline

Delivery occurs automatically within 1 to 5 minutes of payment verification.
• Instant On-Screen Download: Direct download links appear immediately on the post-payment receipt screen.
• Google Drive Cloud Delivery: Private viewer permissions are simultaneously provisioned to the buyer's Google email address via Google Apps Script automation.

3. Non-Receipt of Access

If you have completed payment but have not received access within 15 minutes, please check your email spam/promotions folder or email our 24/7 delivery support team at support@auraexam.com with your Order Reference Number (format: EP-XXXXXX).
